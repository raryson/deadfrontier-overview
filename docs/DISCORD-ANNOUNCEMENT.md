Hey everyone! I've been building **DeadFrontier Overview**, a community companion for Dead Frontier, and I'd love to get more people testing it or contributing.

It has two parts:
• **Browser extension:** quick deposits/withdrawals and buff timers for city pages.
• **Windows app:** an in-game overlay with XP/hour, session history, background loot OCR and estimated item values.

Sessions are saved when the game client closes, and reopening starts a new one. Ordinary equipment uses Yard/NPC values; other items use public market prices. You can also press **Shift + F** for an extra loot snapshot and keep playing while OCR runs in the background.

The interface supports **English and Portuguese**. OCR runs locally, with no screenshot uploads. Loot tracking is still experimental: it logs items seen in the dialog and their potential value, without verifying that they entered your inventory.

If you'd like to try it, report bugs, suggest improvements or contribute code, you're welcome!
